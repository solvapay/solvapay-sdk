Node.js 22 or later is now required by `@solvapay/core`, `@solvapay/server`, `@solvapay/mcp`, `@solvapay/mcp-core`, `@solvapay/server-native` (and its platform packages) and `@solvapay/server-wasm` (`engines.node` is `>=22`). Node 18 and 20 are end-of-life and get no security fixes.
